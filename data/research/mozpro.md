## Pricing Confirmed
- Starter: $49/mo, 1 user, 1 site, 50 keywords, 20K pages crawled
- Standard: $99/mo ($79 annual), 1 user, 3 sites, 300 keywords, 400K pages
- Medium: $179/mo ($143 annual), 2 users, 10 sites, 1500 keywords, 2M pages
- Large: $299/mo ($239 annual), 3 users, 25 sites, 3000 keywords, 5M pages
- Enterprise: custom pricing
- Annual discount: ~20%
- Extra seats: $49/mo each

## Confirmed Claims
- Weekly rank tracking (not daily)
- No JavaScript rendering in crawls
- AI Visibility tracking from Standard plan upward
- Domain Authority, Page Authority, Spam Score metrics
- 7-day free trial (length varies by source - CONFLICT noted)

## Conflicts
- Trial length: some sources report 7 days, others 30 days. Official page doesn't state exact length.
