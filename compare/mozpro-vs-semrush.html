Moz Pro vs Semrush: which is better?

Moz Pro and Semrush are two of the most established SEO platforms, but they take very different approaches. Moz Pro focuses on core organic SEO with a clean interface and industry-standard metrics. Semrush is a broad marketing suite that covers SEO, PPC, content, and social media. This comparison covers pricing, features, data quality, and which tool fits your specific needs.

Moz Pro vs Semrush at a glance

Both platforms cover the core SEO tasks — rank tracking, keyword research, site audits, and backlink analysis — but they differ significantly in scope, pricing, and target audience.

Moz Pro in brief

  • Focus: Core organic SEO only
  • Starting price: $49/month (Starter)
  • Key differentiator: Domain Authority (DA) metric, clean interface, strong educational resources
  • Best for: SEO professionals who want a focused tool without marketing-suite bloat
  • Rank refresh: Weekly
  • Free trial: 7 days on Medium plan

Semrush in brief

  • Focus: Full marketing suite (SEO, PPC, content, social)
  • Starting price: $139/month (Pro)
  • Key differentiator: Broadest feature set, PPC and content marketing tools, larger keyword database
  • Best for: Teams that want one platform for SEO, paid search, and content marketing
  • Rank refresh: Daily
  • Free trial: 7-day trial available

Pricing comparison

Pricing verified November 2026. Prices are in USD. Annual billing gives approximately 17–20% savings on both platforms.

Moz Pro is significantly cheaper at entry level, but Semrush offers more features per dollar. Here is how the plans compare:

Moz Pro pricing

  • Starter — $49/month: 1 user, 1 site, 50 keywords, 20K crawl pages/month
  • Standard — $99/month ($79 annual): 1 user, 3 sites, 300 keywords, 400K crawl pages/month
  • Medium — $179/month ($143 annual): 2 users, 10 sites, 1,500 keywords, 2M crawl pages/month
  • Large — $299/month ($239 annual): 3 users, 25 sites, 3,000 keywords, 5M crawl pages/month

Semrush pricing

  • Pro — $139/month ($117 annual): 1 user, 5 projects, 500 keywords, 10K crawl pages/month
  • Guru — $249/month ($208 annual): 1 user, 15 projects, 1,500 keywords, 300K crawl pages/month, historical data, content tools
  • Business — $499/month ($416 annual): 3 users, 40 projects, 5,000 keywords, 1M crawl pages/month, API access

Verdict on pricing: Moz Pro is cheaper at every tier. Its Standard plan ($99/mo) undercuts Semrush Pro ($139/mo) while offering more tracked keywords (300 vs 500) and more crawl capacity (400K vs 10K pages). However, Semrush includes PPC, content marketing, and social media tools that Moz Pro does not offer at all. If you need only core SEO, Moz Pro gives more value. If you need a broader marketing platform, Semrush justifies its higher price.

Feature-by-feature comparison

Rank Tracking

Both platforms track keyword positions across search engines, but with different update frequencies and capabilities.

  • Moz Pro: Weekly rank updates across Google, Bing, and Yahoo. Supports city-level tracking for local SEO. Provides historical trend data and estimated traffic metrics. Simpler interface with clear reporting.
  • Semrush: Daily rank updates with more granular data. Supports device-specific tracking (desktop/mobile), multi-location tracking, and keyword cannibalization detection (Guru+). More detailed position change alerts and competitive gap analysis.
  • Winner: Semrush for frequency and depth. Moz Pro for simplicity and local SEO tracking.

Keyword Research

  • Moz Pro (Keyword Explorer): Search volume, difficulty scores, organic CTR estimates, and priority metrics. Keyword suggestions organized by topic. SERP analysis showing featured snippets and AI overviews. Solid but smaller keyword database.
  • Semrush (Keyword Magic Tool): Larger keyword database with more long-tail variations. Keyword intent classification, trend data, and competitive density metrics. Topic Research and SEO Writing Assistant (Guru+). More keyword metrics and filtering options.
  • Winner: Semrush for database size and keyword intent features. Moz Pro for clean priority scoring and topic organization.

Site Audits

  • Moz Pro (Site Crawl): User-friendly technical SEO audits with prioritized issue reports. Checks for broken links, duplicate content, missing meta tags, slow pages, and redirect chains. Limited crawl depth on lower plans.
  • Semrush (Site Audit): More comprehensive technical audits with deeper crawl capabilities. Includes on-page SEO checks, core web vitals monitoring, and log file analysis (Business). More granular issue categorization and prioritization.
  • Winner: Semrush for depth and crawl capacity. Moz Pro for ease of use and clear reporting.

Backlink Analysis

  • Moz Pro (Link Explorer): Industry-standard Domain Authority and Page Authority metrics. Link Intersection tool for finding shared backlinks. Competitive Linking Domains analysis. Smaller backlink index than Semrush or Ahrefs.
  • Semrush (Backlink Analytics): Larger backlink database. Authority Score metric. Backlink gap analysis, toxic link detection, and historical backlink data. More detailed anchor text and referring domain analysis.
  • Winner: Semrush for database size and analysis depth. Moz Pro for DA/PA metrics that many stakeholders already understand.

Content Marketing

  • Moz Pro: Limited content marketing features. AI Content Briefs help with on-page optimization suggestions. No content editing, scheduling, or social media tools.
  • Semrush: Comprehensive content marketing suite including Topic Research, SEO Writing Assistant, content template builder, social media scheduling, and content calendar. Looker Studio integration for reporting (Guru+).
  • Winner: Semrush by a large margin. Moz Pro has minimal content marketing capabilities.

PPC and Paid Search

  • Moz Pro: No PPC or paid search features.
  • Semrush: Full PPC toolkit including keyword research for paid search, ad competitor analysis, campaign management tools, and display advertising research.
  • Winner: Semrush exclusively. Moz Pro does not offer PPC features.

Strengths and trade-offs

Choose Moz Pro if

  • You want a focused SEO tool without marketing-suite complexity
  • Domain Authority metrics matter to your stakeholders
  • You prefer a clean, well-documented interface
  • Budget is a primary concern — Moz Pro is cheaper at every tier
  • You value SEO education and guidance alongside tools
  • Weekly rank updates are sufficient for your workflow

Choose Semrush if

  • You need one platform for SEO, PPC, content, and social media
  • Daily rank updates and more granular data matter to you
  • You want the largest keyword and backlink databases
  • Content marketing tools (Writing Assistant, Topic Research) are important
  • You need historical data going back years (Guru+)
  • Your team manages paid search alongside organic SEO

The bottom line

Moz Pro and Semrush serve different types of users. Moz Pro is the better choice if you want a focused, affordable SEO platform with a clean interface and industry-standard metrics. It is especially strong for teams that value Domain Authority, want straightforward rank tracking and site audits, and do not need PPC or content marketing tools. Semrush is the better choice if you need a comprehensive marketing platform that covers SEO, paid search, content marketing, and social media in one subscription. It has larger databases, more frequent data updates, and a broader feature set — but at a higher price and with more complexity. For pure SEO on a budget, Moz Pro wins. For an all-in-one marketing toolkit, Semrush is the clear choice.
