What Circle offers

Circle is an all-in-one community platform that combines discussions, courses, events, and payments. It offers unlimited members on all plans, custom domains, and native course building. Designed for creators, coaches, and brands, it starts at $89/month on annual billing.

Our decision

Circle is a practical choice for creators and brands that want an all-in-one community platform with courses and events. It is not the best default for teams that need a free plan or basic community functionality.

Pricing and plans

Circle charges per month, with annual billing saving approximately 10% over monthly billing. There is no free plan.

Circle plan prices and key limits
PlanAnnual priceMonthly priceKey features
Professional$89/mo$99/moUnlimited members, 3 admins, 20 spaces, 2% transaction fee
Business$199/mo$219/mo5 admins, 30 spaces, 1% transaction fee, workflows
Scale$419/mo$419/mo10 admins, 100 spaces, AI agents, custom SSO

Important billing caveat. Transaction fees apply: 2% on Professional, 1% on Business and Scale. Email Hub costs $99/mo extra for 10K contacts. Live rooms capped at 100 attendees on Professional. Verify current pricing on Circle's official pricing page.

Who should choose Circle?

Good fit

  • Creators that want community, courses, and events in one platform.
  • Coaches that need paid membership functionality.
  • Brands that want custom domain and branding.
  • Agencies that manage multiple client communities.

Look elsewhere if…

  • You need a free plan — Circle offers no free tier.
  • You need email marketing — Email Hub is $99/mo extra.
  • You need live events for 100+ attendees — requires Business tier.
  • You're on a tight budget — $89/mo entry is above market median.

Important limitations

  • Transaction fees on top of plan price.
  • Email Hub is expensive add-on ($99/mo).
  • No free plan or trial clearly advertised.
  • Built-in email functionality is limited.
  • Live rooms capped at 100 attendees on Professional.
