ActiveCampaign overview

ActiveCampaign is a marketing automation platform built around email marketing, customer journeys and audience data. Teams can build automated sequences, segment contacts, personalize campaigns and connect marketing activity with CRM and ecommerce workflows.

Why it may be a good fit

It brings campaigns, segmentation and multi-step automation together, which can reduce manual follow-up when a business has repeat customer journeys.

Who ActiveCampaign is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Businesses sending segmented email campaigns and automated follow-ups
  • Marketing teams managing multi-step customer journeys
  • Teams that need CRM and ecommerce integrations alongside email

Not best for

  • People who only need a basic occasional newsletter
  • Users who require a permanent free plan
  • Teams that cannot justify a paid automation platform or its setup effort

Start with a simpler newsletter service if you do not need branching automations, detailed segmentation or connected sales workflows.

ActiveCampaign pricing

14-day free trial; paid pricing is customized

Official pricing and trial details checked 2026-11-24

ActiveCampaign's current pricing page asks buyers to configure contact volume and currency or request tailored pricing. The official page does not support the old fixed $19, $59 and $99 figures previously shown on this profile, so those legacy prices have been removed.

Free plan: No permanent free plan is advertised. A 14-day free trial is available without a credit card. The Help Center says trial accounts allow up to 100 email sends and one user.

Before choosing a plan, confirm the contact allowance, user seats, automation limits, included channels and billing term in your own quote.
