What the platform covers

Site Explorer

Backlink analysis with the industry's freshest link index. Analyze any domain's backlink profile, find linking pages, and track new and lost links.

Keywords Explorer

Keyword research with search volume, difficulty score, click metrics, and SERP overview. Generate keyword ideas and analyze competitor keywords.

Content Explorer

Content research tool to find top-performing content, analyze what earns links and shares, and identify content gaps.

Site Audit

Technical SEO crawler that identifies crawlability issues, broken links, duplicate content, and on-page SEO problems with prioritized fixes.

Rank Tracker

Weekly keyword position tracking with ranking history, keyword grouping, and SERP feature tracking.

Alerts

Monitor competitor backlinks, keyword rankings, and new content with customizable alerts.

API and MCP access

Programmatic access to Ahrefs data starting from Lite plan. Minimum 50 units per API request.

Who Ahrefs is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • SEO specialists who need backlink data as a primary workflow
  • Link builders who need comprehensive competitor backlink analysis
  • Content strategists using Content Explorer for topic research
  • Agencies managing multiple client sites with recurring SEO needs

Not best for

  • Buyers who need only occasional keyword ideas without backlink analysis
  • Small sites that will not use the full Site Explorer or audit features
  • Teams that need broader marketing tools beyond SEO
  • Budget-conscious buyers who can work with Moz Pro's lower price point

Limitations and cost traps

  • Higher price point than Moz Pro; Lite starts at $129 per month versus Moz's $99 Standard
  • Focused on SEO; does not cover PPC, social media, or broader marketing workflows
  • Lite plan caps at 1,000 credits per user per month and 2,500 rows per report
  • Extra users are expensive: $40-$100 per month depending on plan
  • No free trial; only a 7-day money-back guarantee on annual plans
  • Rank tracking updates weekly, not daily

Ahrefs FAQs

Ahrefs pricing starts at $29 per month for Starter, $129 for Lite ($108 billed annually), $249 for Standard ($208 annually), and $449 for Advanced ($374 annually). Enterprise starts at $1,499 per month with annual commitment.

Ahrefs Free provides limited Site Explorer and Site Audit access for sites you own. The Starter plan at $29 per month offers 200 credits for keyword research.

No free trial. Ahrefs offers a 7-day money-back guarantee on annual plans. Monthly plans have no refund option.

Lite costs $129 per month with 1,000 credits, 6 months of historical data, and 2,500 report rows. Standard costs $249 with unlimited credits, 2 years of history, and 30,000 report rows.

Every plan includes one user. Extra users cost $40 per month on Lite (up to 2), $60 on Standard (up to 5), $80 on Advanced (up to 10), and $100 on Enterprise.

Ahrefs excels at backlink analysis with a fresher index. Semrush offers broader marketing features including PPC and social media. Choose Ahrefs for link-building focus; choose Semrush for broader marketing needs.
