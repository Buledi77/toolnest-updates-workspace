What Aircall offers

Aircall is a cloud phone system designed for sales and support teams. It provides calling, texting, voicemail, call recording, and deep CRM integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive. Plans start at $30/user/month on annual billing, with a 14-day free trial available.

Our decision

Aircall is a practical choice for sales teams that need a clean cloud phone system with deep CRM integrations. It is not the best default for budget-conscious teams or those who need only basic calling.

Pricing and plans

Aircall charges per user, with annual billing saving approximately $6/user/month over monthly billing. There is no free plan; a 14-day free trial is available.
