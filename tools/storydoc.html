Storydoc at a glance

Storydoc is a document creation platform designed for sales teams to build interactive, data-driven proposals, pitch decks, and reports. Unlike static PDFs or PowerPoint files, Storydoc documents are web-based, interactive, and include analytics (who viewed, how long, which sections). It integrates with CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot to pull in live data. It's designed for B2B sales teams who want to replace static proposals with engaging, trackable documents.

Category: Sales

Best for: B2B sales teams creating interactive proposals and pitch decks

Notable: Storydoc's document analytics let you see exactly which sections a prospect engaged with — useful for follow-up conversations.

Pricing and plans

Storydoc offers tiered plans starting around $25/user/month for the Essentials tier, with higher tiers (Growth and Enterprise) adding features like custom branding, advanced analytics, and CRM integrations. Exact pricing may require a demo request.

Billing: Monthly or annual. A free trial is available.

Who Storydoc is best for, and who should skip it

Best for: Interactive document creation and storytelling.

Storydoc fits teams and individuals whose primary workflow matches writing tasks centered on interactive document creation and storytelling.

Key strengths

  • Interactive document creation
  • Embedded media and data
  • Analytics and engagement

Who should skip Storydoc

Skip Storydoc if you need enterprise-grade writing features beyond interactive document creation and storytelling. For more advanced requirements, a dedicated platform in this category may be stronger.

Important limitations

  • Niche use case: Storydoc is specifically for interactive documents. If you need general presentation tools, PowerPoint or Google Slides are more versatile.
  • CRM integration required for full value: The live data features work best when connected to Salesforce or HubSpot. Without a CRM, the platform is less powerful.
  • Template library is limited: While templates are available, they're focused on sales proposals. Non-sales use cases may require custom design work.
  • Not a replacement for design tools: Storydoc is not a graphic design tool. Complex visual designs may require creating assets elsewhere and importing them.
