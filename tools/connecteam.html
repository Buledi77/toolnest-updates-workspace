What Connecteam offers

Connecteam is a workforce management platform that provides employee messaging, scheduling, time clock, and attendance tracking. It offers a free plan for up to 10 users, making it accessible for small businesses. It is widely used by field teams, retail teams, and small businesses.

Our decision

Connecteam is a practical choice for small businesses that want all-in-one workforce management. It is not the best default for large enterprises that need advanced HR features.

Pricing and plans

Connecteam offers a free plan for up to 10 users. Paid plans start at $10/user/month on annual billing.
