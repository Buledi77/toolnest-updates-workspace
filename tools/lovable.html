Lovable review: AI app builder pricing and features

Lovable is an AI-powered full-stack application builder with credit-based billing and cloud hosting. This review covers current plans, credit system, and hosting options.

What Lovable is for

Lovable is an AI-powered full-stack application builder that lets you create, edit, and deploy web apps using natural language prompts. It uses a credit-based system where credits are consumed by AI building, cloud hosting, and AI features in published apps. It is most useful when rapid prototyping or no-code app building is the primary workflow.

Our decision

Lovable is a strong candidate for solo developers building prototypes quickly, non-technical founders validating ideas, and students learning full-stack development. It is a poor value for someone who needs full control over infrastructure or complex custom deployments.

Which Lovable plan fits your workload?

Free is for testing the platform with public projects. Pro ($25/mo) is for solo developers who need private projects and custom domains. Business ($50/mo) adds SSO and data opt-out for teams. Enterprise is for large organizations with custom requirements.

Who Lovable is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Solo developers building prototypes quickly.
  • Non-technical founders validating ideas.
  • Students learning full-stack development.

Not best for

  • Someone who needs full control over infrastructure.
  • A team that requires complex custom deployments.
  • A buyer who cannot justify credit-based billing uncertainty.

Limitations and cost traps

  • Credits consumed by AI building and deployed app runtime.
  • Free plan limited to 5 daily credits (30/month max) with public projects only.
  • Custom domains require Pro plan or higher.
  • No per-seat billing — workspace-based credit system.

Free access and trial reality

Lovable offers a Free plan with 5 daily credits (up to 30 per month) and public projects only. It serves as an entry point for testing the platform, but the limits are not sufficient for regular building work.
