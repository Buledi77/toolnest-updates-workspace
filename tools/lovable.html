Lovable key capabilities

  • AI-powered full-stack application builder
  • Credit-based system for building, hosting, and AI features
  • Cloud hosting included with credits
  • Custom domain support on paid plans
  • Student 50% discount available
  • Shared workspace credits

Lovable important limitations

  • Credits consumed by AI building and deployed app runtime
  • Free plan limited to 5 daily credits (30/month max)
  • Free plan projects are public
  • Custom domains require Pro plan or higher
  • No per-seat billing — workspace-based credit system
