Who Hostinger is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Beginners launching a portfolio, blog or small business site.
  • Freelancers who want a domain, hosting, email and builder in one account.
  • Small stores that have confirmed the plan-specific ecommerce features they need.

Not best for

  • Projects that require dedicated infrastructure, custom networking or strict compliance controls.
  • Teams that need a fully managed site without maintaining content, plugins or settings.
