What Hostinger offers

Hostinger sells web hosting, managed WordPress hosting, cloud hosting, VPS hosting, domains, business email and website-building tools. Its current website also presents an AI Builder with AI-assisted site creation, editing, templates, localization and integrations. The exact feature set depends on the product and plan.

Our decision

Hostinger is a practical shortlist for a beginner, freelancer or small business that wants hosting and site-building tools in one account. It is not the best default for someone who wants a fully managed website service or dedicated infrastructure without operational trade-offs.

Hosting versus the AI Website Builder

Hosting

Hosting is the infrastructure layer for website files, databases, domains, email and applications. Hostinger lists WordPress, PHP/HTML, custom code, CDN, SSL, backups and migration-related features by plan.

  • Choose it when you need control over the site stack.
  • Check storage, websites, backups, mailboxes and resource limits.
  • Keep the old host active during a migration until DNS and email are verified.

AI Builder

Hostinger's official AI Builder page lists AI-assisted website creation, text and image editing, templates, test environments, localization, SEO-ready sites, AI-search visibility, analytics integrations and plan-specific AI credits.

  • Choose it when speed and a guided site workflow matter more than a custom stack.
  • AI credits and ecommerce features vary by plan.
  • Confirm code export, integrations and the features you need before committing.

Ecommerce and business features

Hostinger's pricing comparison marks ecommerce as available on the displayed plans, with ecommerce included on the Unlimited and Cloud Startup examples. The official comparison also lists plan-specific features such as products, payment methods, sales channels, shipping integrations, analytics and checkout controls. Because these are not identical across plans, confirm the exact ecommerce requirements before choosing a tier.

Store fit

Suitable to investigate for a small store or site that needs hosting and a builder together.

AI and marketing

The official pages list AI Builder, AI email marketing on selected plans, analytics and advertising integrations.

Do not assume parity

Do not treat “ecommerce available” as proof that every payment, product, shipping or tax feature is included.

Who should choose Hostinger?

Good fit

  • Beginners launching a portfolio, blog or small business site.
  • Freelancers who want a domain, hosting, email and builder in one account.
  • Small stores that have confirmed the plan-specific ecommerce features they need.
  • WordPress users who want a managed hosting path rather than a bare server.

Look elsewhere if…

  • You need dedicated infrastructure, custom networking or strict compliance controls.
  • You want a fully managed site where you never maintain content, plugins or settings.
  • You need a specialist funnel, course or CRM platform rather than hosting.
  • You require a feature that the selected Hostinger plan does not explicitly include.

Important limitations

  • Promotional rates require long prepaid terms and are not the same as ordinary monthly billing.
  • Renewal prices are materially higher than the introductory equivalents shown above.
  • Backups, storage, sites, mailboxes, AI credits and ecommerce capabilities differ by plan.
  • “Unlimited” features remain subject to Hostinger's Fair Usage Policy.
  • Regional pricing, taxes, currencies, promotions and checkout terms can differ.
  • A builder can speed up launch, but it does not remove the need to plan content, ownership, backups and migration.
