Moz Pro overview (2026)

Moz Pro is an all-in-one SEO platform from Moz, a company that has been a prominent voice in the SEO industry since 2004. It is best known for creating Domain Authority (DA) and Page Authority (PA) — metrics widely used to estimate how well a page or domain might rank in search results. Moz Pro bundles four core tools: Rank Tracker for monitoring keyword positions, Site Crawl (formerly Roger) for technical SEO audits, Keyword Explorer for discovering and analyzing search terms, and Link Explorer for backlink research and competitive link analysis.

The platform is available at moz.com/products/pro. It is cloud-based with no desktop installation required. Moz also offers free tools — MozBar (browser extension), and limited-use Keyword Explorer and Link Explorer queries — so you can evaluate the data before committing to a paid plan.

Core capabilities

  • Rank Tracker — Track keyword positions across search engines and locations, with historical trend data and estimated traffic metrics. Rankings refresh weekly across Google, Bing, and Yahoo, with optional city-level tracking for local SEO.
  • Site Crawl — Crawl your site for technical SEO issues including broken links, duplicate content, missing meta tags, slow pages, and redirect chains. Generates prioritized issue reports.
  • Keyword Explorer — Search volume data, difficulty scores, organic CTR estimates, and click metrics. Provides keyword suggestions, priority scoring, and SERP analysis features.
  • Link Explorer — Backlink discovery, domain authority metrics, link intersection analysis, and competitive gap tools for understanding your link profile versus competitors.

Moz Pro is best suited for SEO professionals, in-house marketing teams, and agencies who want a unified platform for core SEO tasks. It is particularly strong for teams that value Moz's proprietary metrics (DA/PA) and want a clean, well-documented interface.

Moz Pro pricing

Pricing verified November 2026 from moz.com/pricing. Prices are in USD and may change. Annual billing gives approximately 20% savings.

Moz Pro offers four main plans, each increasing limits on tracked sites, keywords, crawl capacity, and user seats:

  • Starter — $49/month (no published annual rate). 1 user, 1 tracked site, 50 tracked keywords, 20,000 pages crawled per month. Core SEO tools including keyword research, competitive research, and MozBar Premium. No free trial on this plan.
  • Standard — $99/month or $79/month billed annually. 1 user, 3 tracked sites, 300 tracked keywords, 400,000 pages crawled per month. Adds unlimited scheduled reports, full backlink analysis, and AI Visibility tracking (150 prompts). Free trial available.
  • Medium — $179/month or $143/month billed annually. 2 users, 10 tracked sites, 1,500 tracked keywords, 2,000,000 pages crawled per month. Adds branded reports, report templates, and higher AI Visibility limits (300 prompts).
  • Large — $299/month or $239/month billed annually. 3 users, 25 tracked sites, 3,000 tracked keywords, 5,000,000 pages crawled per month. Maximum AI Visibility tracking (600 prompts) and highest crawl capacity.

Extra seats cost $49/month each. Enterprise plans with custom pricing are available through Moz sales. Moz offers a 7-day free trial on the Medium plan (monthly billing, card required, one per customer). A free Moz account gives limited Keyword Explorer and 10 Link Explorer queries per month.

Moz Pro: strengths and limitations

After reviewing Moz Pro, we identified the following strengths that make it a compelling option in its category:

What it does well

  • Established metrics — Domain Authority and Page Authority are industry-standard metrics that many clients and stakeholders already understand and reference.
  • Clean interface — Moz Pro has one of the more intuitive and well-documented interfaces among SEO platforms, with clear reporting and helpful tooltips.
  • Link Explorer — The backlink database and analysis tools are robust, with useful features like Link Intersection and Competitive Linking Domains for reverse-engineering competitor strategies.
  • Keyword Explorer — Provides solid keyword data with difficulty scores, priority metrics, and SERP analysis. The keyword suggestion engine is comprehensive.
  • SEO education — Moz has a long history of producing high-quality SEO guides and blog content that helps users understand the reasoning behind their tools.

Where it falls short

  • Weekly rank refresh — Rankings update weekly, not daily. Competitors like Semrush and Ahrefs offer more frequent updates, which matters for fast-moving campaigns.
  • Smaller backlink index — Moz's link database is smaller than Ahrefs' or Semrush's, which can mean fewer discovered backlinks for some sites.
  • Limited content marketing tools — Unlike Semrush, Moz Pro does not include a broad content marketing suite, social media scheduling, or PPC management.
  • Pricing at scale — For agencies managing many sites, the per-campaign cost can add up. The Large plan caps at 25 tracked sites.
  • No PPC or social features — Moz Pro focuses purely on organic SEO. If you need paid search or social media management, you will need additional tools.

How Moz Pro compares

Moz Pro sits in the middle tier of SEO platforms. It is a capable all-in-one tool, but it does not lead in every category:

  • vs Ahrefs — Ahrefs has a larger backlink index and more frequent data updates. Moz Pro is often preferred for its cleaner interface and established DA/PA metrics. Ahrefs is generally stronger for deep backlink research; Moz Pro is often easier for teams new to SEO.
  • vs Semrush — Semrush offers a broader feature set including PPC, content marketing, and social media tools. Moz Pro is more focused on core SEO tasks. If you need an all-in-one marketing platform, Semrush is broader. If you want a focused SEO tool with a clean interface, Moz Pro may be preferable.
  • vs Screaming Frog — Screaming Frog excels at deep technical site audits with granular control. Moz Pro's Site Crawl is more user-friendly but less granular. Many SEOs use both: Screaming Frog for technical deep-dives and Moz Pro for ongoing monitoring.

The bottom line

Moz Pro is a solid choice for SEO professionals and marketing teams who want a focused, well-designed platform for rank tracking, site audits, keyword research, and backlink analysis. Its established metrics (DA/PA), clean interface, and strong educational resources make it accessible for teams new to SEO while still being useful for experienced practitioners. The trade-offs are a smaller backlink index than Ahrefs, weekly rank updates, and a narrower feature set than Semrush. If your primary need is core organic SEO without the bloat of a full marketing suite, Moz Pro is worth evaluating — especially using the free trial to test the workflow against your specific sites and keywords.
