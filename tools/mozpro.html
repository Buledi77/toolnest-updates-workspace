Moz Keyword Explorer pricing

Keyword Explorer is included in every Moz Pro plan — there is no separate Keyword Explorer product or add-on. When you subscribe to Moz Pro, you get Keyword Explorer along with rank tracking, site crawling, and link research.

Which plan context matters

The plan you choose determines your monthly keyword query allowance. Keyword Explorer itself is not gated behind a higher tier, but the number of searches you can run per month varies by plan. Moz publishes these allowances for the three main self-serve tiers:

  • Standard ($99/mo or $79/mo billed annually): 150 keyword queries per month
  • Medium ($179/mo or $143/mo billed annually): 5,000 keyword queries per month
  • Large ($299/mo or $239/mo billed annually): 15,000 keyword queries per month

The $49/mo entry-level plan is described by Moz as an option for individuals managing a single website. Its full query allowance is not listed on the pricing page, so confirm the current limit with Moz before relying on a specific number.

Billing basis

Moz Pro is billed per account, not per keyword. Standard, Medium, and Large plans offer annual billing at a discount of about 20%. Additional user seats cost $49/mo each on any plan level. Moz does not list an annual option for the $49/mo entry plan.

Free or trial access

Moz Pro advertises a free trial; no permanent free plan is stated on the pricing page. The trial gives temporary access to Keyword Explorer and other Moz Pro features.

Important limits

Keyword queries reset monthly. If you exhaust your allowance, you must wait until the next billing cycle or upgrade your plan. For heavy keyword research, the Medium or Large tiers are more practical.

Evidence. Keyword Explorer inclusion and query limits are verified from Moz Pro official pricing on 18 November 2026. Confirm current terms before purchase.
