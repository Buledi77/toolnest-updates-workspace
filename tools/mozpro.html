Moz Pro Pricing (2026): Plans, Limits and What You Actually Pay

Moz Pro is a focused SEO suite from Ziff Davis offering rank tracking, keyword research, site crawling, and link analysis with Domain Authority metrics. Plans start at $49 per month, with annual billing saving approximately 20 percent. What Moz Pro is for

Moz Pro is a focused SEO platform built around four core workflows: Keyword Explorer for research, Link Explorer for backlink analysis, Rank Tracker for position monitoring, and Site Crawl for technical audits. It is best known for Domain Authority and Page Authority metrics, which have become industry shorthand for predicting ranking potential. The platform also includes AI visibility tracking from Standard plan upward, letting you monitor how your brand appears in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini.

Unlike broader marketing platforms, Moz Pro does not include PPC advertising data, social media management, or content marketing automation. It is a deliberate choice: fewer features, lower price, and a cleaner interface for teams whose primary job is search engine optimization.

Our decision

Moz Pro is a strong candidate for an SEO freelancer or small agency managing 1 to 10 client sites who needs recurring rank tracking, keyword research, and site audits at a price below Semrush and Ahrefs. It is a poor value for a team that needs daily rank updates, JavaScript rendering in crawls, or a broad marketing suite with PPC and content tools.

Current Moz Pro plans and pricing

Moz lists four self-serve plans plus custom Enterprise pricing. All plans include access to the same core tools; the differences are in quotas for tracked sites, keywords, crawl pages, and query allowances. Annual billing saves approximately 20 percent on Standard, Medium, and Large plans. Starter does not offer annual billing.

Add-on costs

When plan limits are insufficient, Moz sells add-ons rather than forcing an upgrade:

  • Extra seat: $49 per month per user
  • Extra campaign (tracked site): $10 per month
  • Extra crawl pages: $15 per month per 50,000 pages
  • Extra tracked keywords: $20 per month per 200 keywords
  • Extra keyword queries: $10 per month per 10,000 queries
  • Extra AI Visibility dashboard: $20 per month

The seat cost is the most significant add-on. Adding a second user to Standard costs $49 per month, bringing the effective price to $148 monthly. This makes the Medium plan at $179 (which includes 2 users) better value for two-person teams.

Worked cost scenarios

Freelancer with 2 client sites: Standard at $99 per month covers 3 tracked sites and 300 keywords. If you need a second team member, the extra seat adds $49, totaling $148 per month. Medium at $179 includes 2 users and 10 sites, making it better value if you anticipate growth.

Small agency with 5 sites and 2 staff: Medium at $179 per month ($143 annually) covers 10 sites, 1,500 keywords, and 2 users. This is the most cost-effective option for this workload.

Calculations from listed prices. Verify current add-on pricing on the official site before purchase.

What the platform covers

Keyword Explorer

Keyword research with search volume, difficulty score, organic CTR estimates, and Priority scoring that weights volume, difficulty, and CTR into a single number. The CTR column is a meaningful differentiator: it estimates what percentage of searchers for a given keyword actually click organic results versus clicking ads or being answered by AI Overviews. Shows AI Overviews by keyword.

Link Explorer

Backlink analysis with Domain Authority, Page Authority, and Spam Score metrics. Access to a link index of trillions of links for competitive backlink research. Query allowances vary by plan, from 75 on Starter to 10,000 on Large.

Rank Tracker

Weekly keyword position tracking across Google, Bing, and Yahoo. Supports local tracking by city or postcode. Tracks up to 3 competitors per keyword. Updates are weekly, not daily.

Site Crawl

Automated weekly website crawls that surface technical SEO issues with prioritized recommendations. Crawl capacity ranges from 20,000 pages on Starter to 5 million on Large. No JavaScript rendering.

AI Visibility Tracking

Monitor how your brand appears in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini. Track custom prompts and brand mentions across AI platforms. Available from Standard plan upward.

On-Page Grader

Page-level optimization analysis that identifies content issues and provides prioritized recommendations for improving on-page SEO factors.

Competitive Research

Compare your domain against competitors using Domain Authority, backlink profiles, and keyword overlap analysis to identify opportunities and threats.

Branded Reports

Logo-branded client reports available from Medium plan. Schedule automated reports and customize templates for client delivery.

Which Moz Pro plan fits your workload?

Start with the smallest plan that covers the websites, tracked keywords, and recurring research you actually use. The deciding limit between plans is the tracked-keyword count, not the feature set. For a freelancer tracking their own site plus two or three client projects, Standard is the realistic floor. Upgrade only when higher limits, branded reports, or team workflows solve a real recurring constraint.

Who Moz Pro is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • SEO freelancers tracking 1-3 client sites with recurring keyword and rank monitoring.
  • Small agencies that rely on Domain Authority for client reporting and benchmarking.
  • In-house SEO teams that need core SEO tools without PPC or content marketing features.
  • Users who want AI visibility tracking integrated with traditional SEO workflows.

Not best for

  • Teams that need paid search intelligence or a broad marketing suite beyond SEO.
  • Buyers who require daily rank updates instead of weekly refreshes.
  • Agencies managing 25+ sites without wanting custom Enterprise pricing.
  • Users who need JavaScript rendering capabilities in site crawls.

Limitations and cost traps

  • Rank tracking and site crawls update weekly, not daily. This is sufficient for monthly client reporting but too slow for monitoring launches or rapid ranking changes in real time.
  • No JavaScript rendering in site crawls, limiting analysis of modern single-page applications and dynamic content.
  • Backlink analysis depth trails Ahrefs and Semrush in index freshness and coverage. The link index is large but not as current.
  • Tight query allowances on lower plans. Starter allows only 75 keyword queries per month, which exhausts quickly with active research.
  • Limited content planning capabilities compared to dedicated content strategy tools like Surfer SEO or Semrush's content suite.
  • Extra seats cost $49 per month each, making team expansion expensive on lower tiers. A second user on Standard brings the effective price to $148 per month.

Free access and trial reality

Moz Pro offers a 7-day free trial on Standard plan and above, requiring a credit card. There is no permanent free plan. The trial length varies by source: some report 7 days, others report 30 days. Confirm the exact length on the official trial page before entering card details. Moz also provides free tools separately: the MozBar browser extension for quick Domain Authority checks and limited Link Explorer access with restricted queries.
