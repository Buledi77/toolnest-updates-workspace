Who should skip Asana

Skip Asana if you need enterprise-grade project management with complex dependencies, resource allocation or advanced reporting. For large team coordination, a dedicated project management platform may be stronger.

Limitations and cost traps

Asana is a productivity tool, not an enterprise project management platform. Complex team workflows, resource allocation and advanced reporting may require additional tools. The free or entry plan limits features like guest access, version history or automation.
