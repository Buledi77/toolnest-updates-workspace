Ideogram review: AI image generation pricing and features

Ideogram is an AI image generation platform specializing in legible text rendering within images. This review covers current plans, credit system, and text-in-images capabilities.

What Ideogram is for

Ideogram is an AI image generation platform that specializes in rendering legible text within images, achieving 90–95% text accuracy. It uses a credit-based system with priority and slow credits. It supports character consistency, batch generation, and style references. It is most useful when text-in-images is a core design requirement.

Our decision

Ideogram is a strong candidate for designers needing legible text in AI-generated images, marketers creating posters and ad creatives, and teams needing character consistency. It is a poor value for someone who only needs generic AI images without text.

What Ideogram covers

Text in images

Industry-leading text accuracy at 90–95% for posters, logos, and ad creatives.

Character consistency

Maintain consistent characters across multiple generated images.

Batch generation

Pro and Team plans support batch generation for high-volume workflows.

Style reference

Use up to 3 reference images to guide style and composition.

API access

API available, drawing on the same credits as the web app.

Which Ideogram plan fits your workload?

Free is for trying Ideogram with public images. Plus ($20/mo) is for freelancers with 2,400 credits. Pro ($60/mo) offers the best cost per credit with 7,500 credits and batch generation. Team ($30/user/mo) adds central billing for teams of 2+.

Who Ideogram is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Designers needing legible text in AI-generated images.
  • Marketers creating posters and ad creatives.
  • Teams needing character consistency.

Not best for

  • Someone who only needs generic AI images without text.
  • A buyer who needs full commercial rights clarity before purchase.
  • A team that cannot justify per-user credit allocation.

Limitations and cost traps

  • Free plan limited to 30 credits per week with public generations only.
  • Old Basic plan ($8/mo) retired for new subscribers.
  • Team credits are per-user, not pooled.
  • Enterprise pricing not published.
