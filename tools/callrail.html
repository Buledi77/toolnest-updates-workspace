What CallRail offers

CallRail is a call tracking and lead attribution platform used by 220,000+ businesses. It provides dynamic number insertion, call recording, transcription, and conversation intelligence. Pricing starts at $50/month on annual billing, with usage-based overages for additional numbers, minutes, and texts.

Our decision

CallRail is a practical choice for local businesses and marketing agencies that need to attribute calls to marketing campaigns. It is not the best default for teams that need only basic call tracking or that primarily operate internationally.

Pricing and plans

CallRail charges a base subscription plus usage fees. There is no free plan; a 14-day free trial is available.

Who should choose CallRail?

Good fit

  • Local businesses that need call attribution.
  • Marketing agencies that track campaign performance.
  • Home service companies that receive many calls.
  • Teams that want call recording and transcription.

Look elsewhere if…

  • You need only basic call tracking — simpler tools are cheaper.
  • You primarily operate internationally — CallRail is US/Canada focused.
  • You're on a tight budget — usage fees can significantly increase cost.
  • You need AI features — requires Lead Conversion tier ($150/mo).

Important limitations

  • Usage fees can significantly increase total cost.
  • No free plan.
  • AI features require expensive tiers.
  • Primarily US/Canada/Australia/UK focused.
  • Form tracking only on Complete tiers ($95+/mo).
