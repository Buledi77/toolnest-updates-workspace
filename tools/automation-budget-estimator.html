How it works

Select the tools you want to compare, enter your expected monthly usage, then provide your own pricing assumptions for each tool. The estimator calculates your estimated usage per tool and shows whether it fits within your entered allowance.

How it works: You supply the price, included allowance, and optional overage rate for each tool. The calculator estimates your usage based on runs, steps, and complexity — then compares that usage against your entered allowance. Results are rough estimates based on your input, not verified vendor pricing.
