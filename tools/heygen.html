HeyGen key capabilities

  • AI-powered video generation with custom avatars
  • Credit-based system (not minutes)
  • Avatar IV approximately 20 credits per minute
  • Video translation and dubbing in 175+ languages
  • SCORM export on Business plan
  • API billed separately from subscription credits

HeyGen important limitations

  • Free plan limited to 3 videos/month, 1 minute max, watermarked
  • Credits consumed at different rates depending on model and features
  • Creator plan cannot buy extra credits — must upgrade to Pro
  • Business extra seats do not increase shared credit balance
  • API usage billed separately
