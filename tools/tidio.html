What Tidio offers

Tidio is an AI-powered customer service platform combining live chat, the Lyro AI agent, and automated chatbot flows. It supports Messenger, Instagram, and WhatsApp, and offers a generous free plan with 10 seats. The platform is built for small businesses and e-commerce stores that want to handle customer conversations across multiple channels.

Our decision

Tidio is a practical choice for small businesses that want AI-assisted live chat without paying for a full help desk. It is not the best default for high-volume support teams that need ticketing, SLAs, or unlimited conversation capacity.

Pricing and plans

Tidio offers a free plan with 50 conversations per month and 10 seats. Paid plans start at $29/month (billed annually) for 100 conversations. The Lyro AI agent and Flows chatbot are billed separately from $39/month each.

Who should choose Tidio?

Good fit

  • Small businesses that want AI-assisted live chat.
  • E-commerce stores using Messenger, Instagram, or WhatsApp.
  • Teams that need up to 10 seats on a single plan.
  • Businesses starting with the free plan to test the interface.

Look elsewhere if…

  • You need unlimited conversations — Tidio's limits are tight.
  • You need ticketing and SLAs — Tidio is chat-first, not a help desk.
  • You want all-in-one pricing — Lyro AI and Flows are separate purchases.
  • Your team exceeds 10 seats — Plus tier starts at $300/mo.

Important limitations

  • Three separate billing meters make total cost hard to predict.
  • 10-seat cap on all self-serve plans.
  • Lyro AI conversations are one-time on the free plan, not recurring.
  • Conversation limits exhausted quickly for active businesses.
  • Automatic upgrade at 95% quota usage.
