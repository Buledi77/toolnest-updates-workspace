What Quo offers

Quo (formerly OpenPhone) is a modern business phone system designed for small teams and remote workers. It provides calling, texting, voicemail transcripts, and the Sona AI agent. Plans start at $15/user/month on annual billing, with CRM integrations and advanced features on higher tiers.

Our decision

Quo is a practical choice for small teams that need a clean business phone system with AI features. It is not the best default for large enterprises that need advanced call center features or for teams that primarily need international calling.

Pricing and plans

Quo charges per user, with annual billing saving approximately $10/user/month over monthly billing. There is no free plan; a 7-day free trial is available.

Quo plan prices and key limits
PlanAnnual priceMonthly priceKey features
Starter$15/user/mo$19/user/mo1 number, US/Canada calling, voicemail, Sona AI agent
Business$23/user/mo$33/user/moAI call summaries, phone menus, CRM integrations, HIPAA-ready
Scale$35/user/mo$47/user/moAI call tags, priority support, inbound phone support

Important billing caveat. Additional phone numbers cost $5/month each. International calling and SMS are billed per-use. Verify current pricing on Quo's official pricing page.

Who should choose Quo?

Good fit

  • Small teams that need a clean business phone system.
  • Remote workers who want calling on mobile and desktop.
  • Teams that need HIPAA-ready calling on Business and Scale plans.
  • Businesses that want free number porting.

Look elsewhere if…

  • You need phone menus and CRM integrations — Starter doesn't include them.
  • You primarily make international calls — per-minute fees apply.
  • You need a free plan — Quo offers only a 7-day trial.
  • You need advanced call center features — Quo is built for small teams.

Important limitations

  • Phone menus, call transfers, CRM integrations start on Business ($23/mo).
  • International calling incurs per-minute fees.
  • Fair usage policy on calling not clearly defined.
  • Sona AI agent billed separately beyond included credits.
  • Additional numbers cost $5/mo each.
