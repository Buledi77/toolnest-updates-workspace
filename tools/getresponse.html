What GetResponse offers

GetResponse is an all-in-one email marketing platform that combines email campaigns, marketing automation, landing pages, and webinars. Founded in 1998, it offers a generous free plan, unlimited emails on paid plans, and a 14-day premium trial. It also provides a 50% discount for certified nonprofit organizations.

Our decision

GetResponse is a practical choice for small businesses and creators who want email, landing pages, and webinars in one platform. It is not the best default for someone who needs advanced e-commerce email flows on the entry plan or unlimited automation workflows at the lowest price.

Pricing and plans

GetResponse offers a free plan and four paid tiers. Pricing scales with contact count. Annual billing saves 18% over monthly billing.

Who should choose GetResponse?

Good fit

  • Small businesses that want email, landing pages, and webinars in one platform.
  • Creators who need a free plan to start building an audience.
  • Nonprofits that qualify for the 50% discount.
  • Marketers who want unlimited email sends on paid plans.

Look elsewhere if…

  • You need automation workflows on the free plan — GetResponse free has no automation.
  • You need unlimited automations on the entry plan — Starter is limited to 1 workflow.
  • You need webinars or online courses — these require the Creator plan.
  • You need SMS marketing or transactional email — these require Enterprise.

Important limitations

  • Free plan has no automation workflows or A/B testing.
  • Starter plan is limited to 1 automation workflow.
  • Advanced features (webinars, courses, paid newsletters) require Creator plan.
  • Pricing scales with contact count — costs rise as your list grows.
  • Enterprise pricing is not published.
  • NGO discount requires certification.
