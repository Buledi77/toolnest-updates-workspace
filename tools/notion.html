Notion key capabilities

  • One home for notes and to-dos
  • Templates to start fast
  • Good for solo work

Notion important limitations

  • 5 MB file upload limit on Free plan restricts media-heavy workflows
  • 7-day page history on Free plan means accidental deletions older than a week cannot be recovered
  • Notion AI features require Business plan ($20/user/month) — not available on Plus
  • No native email or calendar integration; relies on third-party connections
  • Offline access is limited; requires internet connection for full functionality

Who should choose Notion

  • solo professionals and knowledge workers
  • small teams (2-20 people) building wikis and project trackers
  • startups using Notion as an all-in-one workspace
  • students and educators
  • content teams managing documentation and databases
