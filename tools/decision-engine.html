Scoring methodology

Each tool is scored out of 90 points using these transparent rules:

Use Case Match (40 points)

How many of your selected use cases match the tool's verified use case tags.

Category Match (20 points)

Whether the tool belongs to one of your selected categories.

Best For Match (20 points)

How many of your selected use cases match the tool's "best for" descriptions.

Platform Match (10 points)

Whether the tool is available on your selected platforms.
