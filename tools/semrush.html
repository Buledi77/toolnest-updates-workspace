What Semrush is for

Semrush is a broad SEO and AI-search visibility platform. Its current SEO pricing page lists keyword research, competitor analysis, Position Tracking, Site Audit and AI-search capabilities. It is most useful when search research is a recurring business workflow rather than an occasional lookup.

Our decision

Semrush is a strong candidate for an SEO freelancer, agency, content team or in-house marketer who will use several research workflows. It is a poor value for a site owner who needs only occasional keyword ideas or a single technical crawl.

SEO + AI Search versus separate AI Visibility

Semrush's core pricing page now places AI-search features inside the SEO plan family, including AI-search performance, sentiment, visibility reports and custom prompts. Semrush also lists a separate AI Visibility Base plan at $99/month per domain billed annually.

Core SEO + AI Search plans

Use these when you want SEO research, Position Tracking and Site Audit alongside the AI-search features listed for the selected tier. Prompt, domain, report and website allowances vary by plan.

AI Visibility Base

The official AI pricing page lists 25 custom prompts tracked daily, one brand-performance domain, mentions from ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini and Perplexity, competitor/prompt research and an AI-readiness site audit. It is priced per domain and is not the same as every core-plan allowance.

What the platform covers

Keyword research

Research and optimization tools are included in the current SEO tiers, with plan-specific daily keyword limits.

Competitor research

The official plan page lists competitor insights and broader market visibility as a core positioning.

Site auditing

Site Audit is listed in the core SEO plans; Starter also lists an AI-ready Site Audit.

Rank tracking

Position Tracking is listed for the SEO plan family, with daily tracked-keyword quotas by tier.

Content and cannibalization

Pro+ lists content optimization and keyword cannibalization analysis.

API and reporting

Advanced lists advanced API data integration, team users and reporting. Separate Base Report and Pro Report add-ons are also listed.

Which Semrush plan fits your workload?

Start with the smallest plan that covers the websites, tracked keywords and recurring research you actually use. Upgrade only when higher limits, historical data, AI-search tracking, reporting or team workflows solve a real recurring constraint.

Who Semrush is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • SEO freelancers and agencies with recurring client research.
  • Content teams that need competitor, keyword and optimization workflows.
  • Organizations monitoring multiple websites, locations or markets.
  • Teams that can use AI-search visibility data and prompt tracking in a defined workflow.

Not best for

  • Someone who only needs an occasional keyword idea.
  • A small site that will not use more than a few reports or projects.
  • A team that cannot justify the plan or add-on cost.
  • A buyer who needs a single specialist crawler rather than a broad platform.

Limitations and cost traps

  • Website monitoring, daily tracked keywords, AI prompts, reports and other quotas are plan-specific.
  • Advanced API access, team users and reporting are listed at the Advanced or add-on level, not assumed in every plan.
  • The separate AI Visibility Base plan is billed per domain annually, so multi-domain coverage changes the cost.
  • The platform's breadth can create a learning and governance burden for small teams.
  • Free access is capped evaluation access, not a substitute for a paid client workflow.
  • Search-volume, traffic and visibility data are estimates or vendor measurements; use them comparatively rather than as exact real-world counts.

Free access and trial reality

Semrush currently lists a /bin/bash free option with no credit card required, one demo project and 10 reports per day across vital tools. Paid SEO tiers also advertise trial access. Treat the free option as an evaluation route, not a replacement for the higher limits of paid plans.
