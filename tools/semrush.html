Semrush overview

Semrush is an SEO and online-visibility platform. Its core jobs are finding keywords, tracking rankings, auditing a site, analysing competitors and backlinks, and monitoring how a brand appears in AI search results. Plans differ mainly by the number of websites, tracked keywords, AI prompts and reports included.

Why it may be a good fit

One subscription covers keyword research, rank tracking, site audits, competitor analysis and AI-visibility monitoring.

Who Semrush is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Marketers who need keyword research, rank tracking, site audits and competitor analysis in one place.
  • Agencies and in-house teams managing several websites and needing shared reporting.
  • Teams that also want to monitor AI-search visibility, sentiment and custom prompts.
  • Buyers who value breadth and are willing to pay for a full platform rather than separate tools.

Not best for

  • Solo users who only need occasional keyword ideas and want the lowest monthly cost.
  • Sites whose main need is a technical crawler; a dedicated crawler can be cheaper and more focused.
  • Buyers who will not use competitor, backlink or AI-visibility features.
  • Teams that need a permanent free plan rather than evaluation access.

Semrush key capabilities

  • Keyword research and keyword gap analysis
  • Daily rank tracking and position monitoring
  • Site Audit and technical SEO checks
  • Backlink analysis and competitor research
  • Content optimisation and cannibalisation analysis
  • AI visibility reports, sentiment monitoring and custom prompt tracking
  • Reporting, MCP access and API options on higher tiers

Semrush important limitations

  • The entry paid plan is $139/mo, which is more than some focused SEO tools charge for a starter tier.
  • Each paid plan includes one user by default; additional users are a paid add-on starting at $45/mo.
  • AI-visibility limits are plan-bound: Starter covers 1 domain and 50 prompts per day, while Pro+ covers 100 prompts per day.
  • The free account is evaluation access with 1 demo project and 10 reports per day, not a permanent working plan.
  • This is a research-based profile built from official pricing and feature pages; ToolNest has not run a hands-on lab test of every feature.

Who should choose Semrush

  • You want keyword research, rank tracking, site audits, backlinks and competitor analysis under one login.
  • You manage multiple sites and need reporting across them; Pro+ lists 15 websites and Advanced lists 40+.
  • You need AI-search visibility monitoring alongside classic SEO metrics.
  • You will use the breadth often enough to justify a platform subscription.

Who should skip Semrush

  • You only need a handful of keyword lookups per month; a lower-cost starter plan may be enough.
  • You mainly need to crawl a site for technical errors; a dedicated crawler can be more focused.
  • You need a permanent free plan with meaningful monthly limits.
  • You will not use competitor, backlink or AI-visibility features.
