Who should skip Figma

Skip Figma if you need professional-grade vector illustration, photo retouching or print production. For advanced creative work, dedicated design software is the stronger route.

Figma is designed for a specific type of design work. It is not a replacement for professional creative software. Complex projects, advanced typography and print production may hit limits. Check the plan for team features and asset storage.
