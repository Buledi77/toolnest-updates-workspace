What Keap offers

Keap (formerly Infusionsoft) is an all-in-one CRM and marketing automation platform built for small businesses. It combines contact management, email marketing, sales pipelines, invoicing, and payments in a single platform. With 200,000+ users since 2001, it offers deep automation workflows but at a premium price.

Our decision

Keap is a practical choice for service businesses that want CRM, marketing, and payments in one platform. It is not the best default for budget-conscious teams or those who need only basic CRM functionality.

Pricing and plans

Keap charges based on contacts and users, with a required one-time implementation fee. There is no free plan; a 14-day free trial is available.

Important billing caveat. A $500 one-time implementation fee is required. Extra users cost $39/mo each. Contact tiers increase price significantly. An early exit fee applies on yearly contracts. Verify current pricing on Keap's official pricing page.

Who should choose Keap?

Good fit

  • Service businesses that want all-in-one CRM and marketing.
  • Small businesses that need invoicing and payments built in.
  • Teams that value deep automation workflows.
  • Businesses that want a single platform for CRM, marketing, and sales.

Look elsewhere if…

  • You're on a tight budget — Keap starts at $299/mo.
  • You need only basic CRM — simpler tools are more affordable.
  • You need a free plan — Keap offers only a 14-day trial.
  • You're concerned about billing — Trustpilot rating is 1.1/5.

Important limitations

  • High starting price at $299/mo.
  • Required $500 implementation fee.
  • Steep price increase for additional contacts.
  • Trustpilot rating 1.1/5 — billing and cancellation complaints.
  • Early exit fee on yearly contracts.
