Bolt.new review: browser-based app builder pricing and features

Bolt.new is a browser-based full-stack application builder by StackBlitz with token-based billing and built-in hosting. This review covers current plans, token system, and hosting options.

What Bolt.new is for

Bolt.new is a browser-based full-stack application builder by StackBlitz. It uses a token-based billing system where tokens are consumed by reading project files and writing code. It includes built-in hosting on bolt.host addresses with custom domain support on paid plans. It is SOC 2 Type II compliant. It is most useful when rapid browser-based prototyping is the primary workflow.

Our decision

Bolt.new is a strong candidate for developers wanting browser-based full-stack development, teams needing rapid prototyping, and startups building MVPs quickly. It is a poor value for someone who needs full control over infrastructure or complex custom deployments.

Current Bolt.new plans and pricing

Bolt.new uses token-based billing. Free tokens expire at month end with no rollover. Paid tokens roll over for up to two months. Yearly billing saves up to 28%.

What Bolt.new covers

Browser-based building

Full-stack app development entirely in the browser — no local setup required.

Built-in hosting

Deploy apps to bolt.host addresses with included hosting on every plan.

Custom domains

Connect custom domains on Pro plan and above.

Token rollover

Team collaboration

Teams plan adds central billing, admin controls, and private NPM registry support.

Which Bolt.new plan fits your workload?

Free is for trying Bolt with public projects. Pro ($25/mo) is for individuals who need custom domains and no Bolt branding. Teams ($30/member/mo) adds centralized management. Enterprise is for large organizations with SSO and compliance needs.

Who Bolt.new is best for, and who should skip it

Best for

  • Developers wanting browser-based full-stack development.
  • Teams needing rapid prototyping.
  • Startups building MVPs quickly.

Not best for

  • Someone who needs full control over infrastructure.
  • A team that requires complex custom deployments.
  • A buyer who cannot justify token-based billing uncertainty.

Limitations and cost traps

  • Free plan limited to 1M tokens/month with 300K daily cap.
  • Token usage increases as project size grows.
  • Free tokens do not roll over.
  • Custom domains require Pro plan or higher.
  • Bolt branding on Free plan sites.
