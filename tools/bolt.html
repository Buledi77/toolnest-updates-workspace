Bolt.new key capabilities

  • Browser-based full-stack application builder by StackBlitz
  • Token-based billing system
  • SOC 2 Type II compliance
  • Built-in hosting on bolt.host addresses
  • Custom domain support on Pro and above
  • Token rollover for up to two months on paid plans

Bolt.new important limitations

  • Free plan limited to 1M tokens/month with 300K daily cap

  • Token usage increases as project size grows
  • Free tokens do not roll over
