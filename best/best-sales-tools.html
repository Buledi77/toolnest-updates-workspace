Which pick fits your situation

You want to sell a course, product or email list simply

Systeme.io — Sales pages, email lists and online course hosting in one account remove the need for separate marketing tools.

You need a full CRM with marketing and sales workflows

HubSpot — CRM, marketing automation and sales pipelines in one platform suit businesses that need structured customer management.

The bottom line

Systeme.io covers the basics — sales pages, email and courses — at a lower cost. HubSpot is for when you need a full CRM with marketing automation and sales pipelines. Start simple with Systeme.io; scale to HubSpot when customer management becomes complex.

HubSpot

Sales

HubSpot is here for the reason that it goes beyond email marketing into full CRM, sales pipeline and marketing automation. If the job is not just sending campaigns but tracking leads, scoring contacts and aligning marketing with sales, HubSpot provides the connected workflow. The free CRM entry point lets teams start without a budget.

Best for: CRM, marketing and sales workflows in one platform

  • Free CRM entry point
  • Marketing automation
  • Sales pipeline tools

Watch out: The marketing and sales features that matter most sit behind higher-tier plans. For pure email campaigns at low volume, Mailchimp or Brevo may be simpler and cheaper. Verify which features are included at each tier before committing.
