Which pick fits your situation

You need social graphics fast, without design training

Canva — The template library and drag-and-drop editor let anyone produce posts, presentations and simple brand assets in minutes.

Your job is interface design or prototyping

Figma — Real-time collaboration, interactive prototypes and reusable design systems make it the standard for product design teams.

You need quick social content with Adobe integration

Adobe Express — It bridges simple social graphics with Adobe font and stock access, useful if your brand already uses Adobe tools.

You only need to remove image backgrounds

remove.bg — It does one job automatically — no editor, no templates, just upload and download a clean cutout.

The bottom line

If you need speed and simplicity, start with Canva. Move to Figma when the job is interface design or team collaboration. Adobe Express fits if your brand already uses Adobe tools. Use remove.bg only when background removal is the sole task.

Best Design tools

A practical shortlist organized by the job each design product fits best.

Last reviewed October 2026How ToolNest reviews tools

Category shortlist

1

Canva

Design

Canva leads this shortlist for the reason that it removes the skill barrier for everyday design. A person who has never opened a design application can produce social posts, presentations and simple brand assets in minutes using the template library and drag-and-drop editor. For teams, brand kits keep output consistent without a dedicated designer.

Best for: Social graphics, presentations and simple brand designs

  • Large template library
  • Easy drag-and-drop editor
  • Useful for teams and brand kits

Watch out: It is not a replacement for professional vector or layout software. Complex illustrations, multi-page print layouts and advanced typography will hit limits quickly. Check the Pro plan for team seats and asset storage before committing.

See Canva alternatives
2

Figma

Design

Figma earns its position for the reason that collaborative interface design is its core strength. Multiple people can work on the same file in real time, prototypes are interactive without exporting, and design systems with reusable components scale across a team. If the job is product design rather than social graphics, Figma is the natural starting point.

Best for: Collaborative interface design and prototyping

  • Real-time collaboration
  • Interactive prototypes
  • Reusable design systems

Watch out: It is specialised for interface work. Marketing graphics, photo editing and print design are not its focus. The free tier limits team features; verify the plan for version history and asset export before a team depends on it.

See Figma alternatives
3

Adobe Express

Design

Adobe Express sits here for the reason that it bridges quick social content with Adobe's asset ecosystem. A user who already has Adobe stock photos, fonts or Creative Cloud files can pull them directly into Express templates. For short-form video, social posts and branded one-pagers, it is faster than opening a full Creative Cloud application.

Best for: Quick social graphics, videos and brand content

  • Ready-made templates
  • Simple editing tools
  • Adobe asset connections

Watch out: It shares overlap with Canva in the template space. If you do not need Adobe asset integration, Canva's broader template library may be the stronger fit. Check the current plan limits for premium templates and brand kit features.

See Adobe Express alternatives
4

remove.bg

Design

remove.bg is included for the reason that background removal is a narrow job that it handles in one click. When a workflow requires clean cutouts for product photos, composites or listings, it eliminates the manual masking step. The API makes it easy to integrate into automated pipelines.

Best for: Removing image backgrounds quickly

  • Automatic cutouts
  • Fast web workflow
  • API available

Watch out: It is a single-purpose tool. For broader image editing, Canva or Adobe Express cover more ground. The free tier limits resolution; check the paid plan for high-resolution downloads and API call volume.
