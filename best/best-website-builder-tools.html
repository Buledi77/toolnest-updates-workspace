Which pick fits your situation

You want a modern landing page or design-led marketing site

Framer — Visual responsive design, animations and fast publishing suit designers who want control without code.

You need a visual website with CMS and design control

Webflow — Visual design with CMS collections and interactions, suitable for agencies and designers who need content management.

You want a polished portfolio or small business site

Squarespace — Templates and simplicity make it fast to launch a professional-looking site without design expertise.

The bottom line

Framer suits designers who want visual control and fast publishing. Webflow adds CMS and deeper design control for agencies. Squarespace is the fastest route to a polished site with templates. Pick based on how much design control you need versus how fast you need to launch.

Category shortlist

1

Framer

Website Builder

Framer leads for the reason that it combines visual design with fast publishing and hosting in one workflow. A designer or marketer can build a responsive landing page, add animations and interactions, and publish without touching code. For design-led marketing websites that need to launch quickly, Framer removes the development bottleneck.

Best for: Modern landing pages and design-led marketing websites

  • Visual responsive design
  • Fast publishing and hosting
  • Strong animation and interaction tools

Watch out: It is optimised for marketing sites and landing pages, not complex web applications or large content sites. For CMS-heavy sites, Webflow may be stronger. Check the visitor limits and custom domain rules on the current plan.

See Framer alternatives
2

Webflow

Website Builder

Webflow earns its position for the reason that it provides visual web design with a built-in CMS and managed hosting. A team that needs a content-managed website with design control — blogs, resource centres, product pages — can build and maintain it without a development team. The CMS structure is more flexible than Framer's for content-heavy sites.

Best for: Visual websites with CMS and design control

  • Visual web design
  • Built-in CMS
  • Managed hosting

Watch out: The learning curve is steeper than template-based builders. For simple portfolios or one-page sites, Squarespace or Framer may be faster. Verify the CMS item limits and bandwidth at each tier.

See Webflow alternatives
3

Squarespace

Website Builder

Squarespace is here for the reason that its templates produce polished websites and portfolios with minimal effort. A freelancer, artist or small business can launch a professional site in a day using the built-in templates, commerce tools and managed hosting. For users who prioritise visual quality over custom design control, Squarespace delivers consistently.

Best for: Polished websites and portfolios with templates

  • Professional templates
  • Built-in commerce
  • Managed hosting

Watch out: Custom design control is limited compared to Webflow or Framer. Complex layouts and advanced interactions require workarounds. Check the commerce transaction fees and storage limits on the current plan.
