Category shortlist

1

Perplexity

Research

Perplexity leads for the reason that it grounds answers in visible source links. A researcher, student or professional who needs to verify claims can see the sources behind each answer. The follow-up questions keep a research thread moving without losing context. For web research that requires traceable references, Perplexity is the practical starting point.

Best for: Web research with visible source links

  • Source-led answers
  • Fast topic summaries
  • Useful follow-up questions

Watch out: It is a research assistant, not a replacement for dedicated reference management or academic databases. The Pro plan adds search limits and model access; verify the current tier before heavy use.

See Perplexity alternatives
2

NotebookLM

Research

NotebookLM earns its position for the reason that it works with your own sources rather than the open web. A student or researcher can upload documents, articles or notes and ask questions that are grounded in that material. The source-grounded approach reduces hallucination when the job is studying or analysing specific materials.

Best for: Studying and asking questions across your own sources

  • Source-grounded notebooks
  • Document summaries
  • Research organization

Watch out: It is designed for personal research notebooks, not team collaboration or publication workflows. The source material quality directly affects output quality. Check the current document limits and format support.
