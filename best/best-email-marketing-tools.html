Which pick fits your situation for Best Email Marketing tools: Ranked List

You publish a newsletter and want to monetize it

beehiiv — Built-in paid subscriptions, referral growth tools and a publishing editor are designed for newsletter-first creators.

You are a blogger or podcaster selling digital products

Kit — Creator-focused automations, digital product funnels and WordPress integration fit content creators who sell directly to their audience.

You run email campaigns for a business

Mailchimp — Audience segmentation, basic automation and a large template library cover standard business email marketing needs.

You need email plus SMS and transactional messaging

Brevo — It combines marketing email, SMS campaigns and transactional messaging in one account, useful for customer communication beyond newsletters.

The bottom line for Best Email Marketing tools: Ranked List

If you publish a newsletter, start with beehiiv. Kit is the better fit if you sell digital products to an audience. Mailchimp covers standard business email campaigns. Brevo makes sense when you need SMS and transactional messaging alongside email.

Mailchimp: review and fit for Best Email Marketing tools: Ranked List

Email Marketing

Mailchimp leads for the reason that it combines campaign building, audience segmentation and basic automation in one account. A small business or solo marketer can build a list, design an email and set up a simple welcome sequence without switching tools. The free tier lets you evaluate the workflow before paying.

Best for: Email campaigns, basic automation and audience management

  • Campaign builder
  • Audience segmentation
  • Marketing automations

Watch out: As contact counts grow, the pricing jumps. Advanced automation and multi-channel campaigns require higher tiers. If you need deep CRM integration, HubSpot may be the stronger route. Verify current contact limits and sending caps on the pricing page.

See Mailchimp alternatives
4

Brevo: review and fit for Best Email Marketing tools: Ranked List

Email Marketing

Brevo earns its position for the reason that it bundles email, SMS and contact management at a price point that suits growing teams. The automation builder covers welcome flows, abandoned cart sequences and re-engagement campaigns. For businesses that want multi-channel communication without separate SMS providers, Brevo consolidates the stack.

Best for: Email, SMS and customer communication campaigns

  • Email automation
  • SMS options
  • Contact management

Watch out: The interface has a steeper learning curve than Mailchimp for beginners. Template design is functional rather than polished. Check the email sending limits per tier and SMS pricing before scaling.
